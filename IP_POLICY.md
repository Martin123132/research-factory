# Rights and intellectual-property policy

Status: **draft for review before public launch**.

This policy preserves attribution and provenance without turning the Research
Factory into the owner of other people's work. It is a project rule, not a
legal opinion or a substitute for advice about a particular invention.

## Core rule: no assignment to the Factory

A contributor retains every copyright, patent, database, design and other
right that they lawfully hold in their contribution. Submitting work does not
assign those rights to the Research Factory, its repository owner, its
maintainers, its validators or its agents.

That rule cannot create rights the contributor does not have. Employment,
university, funding, collaboration, prior agreements and third-party licences
may affect who owns a right or who may grant permission. Contributors must
declare those interests; the Factory records the declaration but does not
certify it.

## Different things stay different

The Factory records these separately:

- **scientific content:** facts, measurements, equations, theorem statements,
  hypotheses and conclusions;
- **copyright material:** code, diagrams, prose, datasets with protectable
  selection or arrangement, and other expression;
- **patent interests:** potentially patentable implementations or applications;
- **credit:** who did which intellectual, experimental, software, validation
  and review work;
- **external rewards:** prizes, grants, employment credit and commercial return.

The Factory will not assert exclusive ownership over a fact, mathematical
relationship, observation, theorem statement or law of nature as such. That
does not erase rights that may exist in a particular implementation,
invention, dataset, drawing or written explanation.

## Required contribution declaration

Every substantive submission must identify one accountable human and declare:

1. the contributor has the right to submit every included component;
2. all material sources and applicable licences are recorded;
3. employer, university, funder and collaborator interests have been checked;
4. material human and AI assistance is disclosed;
5. the public package contains no confidential information or trade secret;
6. any patent-sensitive work was dealt with before public upload; and
7. possible joint inventors and joint authors were considered rather than
   silently omitted.

A false or materially incomplete declaration can block or retract a merge. It
does not make the Factory the replacement owner.

## Provenance for derived work

Derived work must carry a dependency record identifying the exact earlier
artifacts, versions, people, licences and permissions used. A later contributor
may own new material they created while still needing permission for earlier
material. A validator, maintainer or repository owner does not become an author
or inventor merely by performing that role.

## No confidential submissions

The source repository is a public-record workflow even while its GitHub
visibility is private. Do not rely on it as an NDA room, patent attorney file or
trade-secret store. Material that cannot yet be disclosed must not be uploaded.
See [PATENTS_AND_PUBLIC_DISCLOSURE.md](PATENTS_AND_PUBLIC_DISCLOSURE.md).

## Licensing is a separate action

Ownership and permission are different. Contributors keep the rights they hold
but a licence is still needed for other people to copy, run, validate, modify
or redistribute protected material. The proposed path-by-path licensing model
and its current inactive status are in [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md).

## Scientific standing is rights-neutral

An evidence package passes or fails only under its workbench contract. Patent
intent, commercial terms, credentials, popularity and adoption of a voluntary
pledge cannot improve or reduce a scientific score. A rights dispute can pause
publication or distribution, but it cannot rewrite measurements.

## Disputes and corrections

Maintainers may temporarily quarantine a package when there is a specific,
documented rights concern. The public record should preserve the reason,
affected artifact hashes and eventual outcome without publishing confidential
legal material. Credit and provenance corrections remain append-only whenever
practical; they must not silently rewrite history.
