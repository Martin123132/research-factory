# Third-party data boundary

This repository records references, expected identifiers and acquisition
instructions for external scientific datasets. It does not assert a right to
redistribute a dataset merely because the dataset is publicly downloadable.

Before adding third-party files:

1. record the authoritative source and retrieval date;
2. confirm the redistribution licence or obtain permission;
3. store raw and interpreted content hashes;
4. preserve the dataset's required attribution and notices;
5. keep hidden evaluation material outside the public source repository.

In particular, official TSPLIB instances are referenced but not bundled until
their redistribution basis is established. Concorde binaries and source are not
vendored into the practical runner.
