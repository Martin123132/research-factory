# Starter-pack construction rule — ${WORKBENCH_CODE}

The entry exercise is credential-neutral: anyone may attempt it, but nobody bypasses it.

A runnable pack must declare prerequisites, licensed acquisition steps, frozen public fixtures and hashes, a safe reference command, expected public output, a verifier or controlled review protocol, an evidence-package layout, a negative-result route, a timebox and a safety notice.

Passing proves only that the person and agent can follow the station method. It is not evidence for the open problem and it does not count as an independent reproduction.

Until every required item exists, the pack remains `BRIEF_ONLY` and the station cannot accept live research submissions.
