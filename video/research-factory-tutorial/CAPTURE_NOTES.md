# Capture notes

## Homepage screenshots

The five viewport captures show a restrained industrial interface: a warm paper grid surrounds near-black machinery panels, while safety orange marks active controls and green marks safe zero-state readings. The layout uses large left-anchored headlines, instrument readouts, ruled cards, and generous spacing; it feels like an engineering operations manual made navigable.

## Design tokens

The primary palette is `#171A18`, `#F2EFE6`, `#FFFDF7`, `#E95C2B`, `#A83E1C`, `#23755B`, and `#C9C3B6`. Typography is Geist (variable, 100–900) for statements and Geist Mono (variable, 100–900) for identifiers, states, measurements, and boundary labels. The capture contains five homepage sections, ten headings, and two primary calls to action.

## Visible text

The content hierarchy is explicit: one hundred stations, one evidence standard, a measurable station map, one instrumented test article, and a locked live-research lane. The strongest tutorial language is already present in the product: construct, commission, retain, and no scientific promotion.

## Assets

The site has no downloadable photographic or illustration assets, so the useful video assets are the captured route screenshots themselves. The most useful views are the homepage readiness panel, station directory, WB-001 hard gate, shift board, runner trust boundary, append-only history, and one-way system boundary.

## Motion

The captured product intentionally has almost no native animation beyond interface transitions. The tutorial should add measured camera movement, callout bubbles, path drawing, counters, and controlled push or focus transitions without changing the product's calm engineering character.
