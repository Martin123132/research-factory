# Credit, authorship and prizes

Scientific credit follows recorded contribution, not repository authority,
credentials or ownership of the infrastructure.

## Contribution ledger

Each promoted package must include a versioned role ledger naming the people
who materially performed each applicable role:

- problem formulation;
- methodology;
- software or formalisation;
- investigation or experiment;
- data or benchmark curation;
- analysis;
- original draft and visualisation;
- independent reproduction;
- discrepancy diagnosis; and
- supervision, administration or funding.

The ledger records contribution; it does not decide legal inventorship. A
person can appear in several roles, subject to the Factory's independence rules.
Its closed machine vocabulary is
[`factory/workbench_standard/schema/contribution-ledger-v1.schema.json`](factory/workbench_standard/schema/contribution-ledger-v1.schema.json).

## Boundaries

- Opening the workbench does not make the repository owner an author.
- Maintaining infrastructure does not make a maintainer a solver.
- Funding compute does not by itself make a funder an author or inventor.
- Running an independent reproduction earns validation credit, not automatic
  authorship of the original result.
- Reviewing prose or approving a pull request is not independent reproduction.
- An AI system is recorded as a tool with its material role and configuration;
  the accountable humans and applicable law determine human attribution.

Acknowledgement is appropriate where a contribution is real but below the
authorship threshold used by the eventual journal, institute or prize body.

## Major claims

Before the Factory labels a result a major breakthrough, a conflict-free credit
panel must compare the role ledger, artifact history, sealed timestamps and
prior work. Proposed attribution is published for correction before promotion.
Disputes pause the attribution label, not the underlying evidence record.

## External prizes

External award bodies decide eligibility, claimant identity, deadlines and
distribution under their own rules. The Factory cannot promise that a package
qualifies, file a claim on somebody's behalf, or rewrite those rules.

The Research Factory, repository owner and maintainers take **no automatic
percentage or ownership share** of a prize because work used the Factory.
Contributors who collaborate should make a written prize-sharing agreement
early, before a valuable result exists. The agreement should distinguish the
original solver, later contributors, validators and infrastructure work, and it
must not buy scientific approval.

If collaborators have no agreement, the Factory preserves the ledger and
evidence but does not invent a financial split.

## Corrections

Credit changes require a reason, evidence and an append-only correction. No
person may be silently removed. Fraudulent attribution, fabricated identity or
concealed conflict can trigger quarantine or retraction under governance.
