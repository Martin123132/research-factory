# Open Factory Charter

Status: **version 1.0 — adopted for Factory construction and synthetic
commissioning**.

The Research Factory exists to help people and their agents investigate large,
measurable problems without surrendering ownership, dignity or scientific
standards. It is public infrastructure, not an authority that can manufacture
truth by accepting a pull request or announcing a result.

## Constitutional purpose

The Factory must make rigorous research easier to enter, safer to perform,
cheaper to reproduce and harder to fake. It must preserve useful failure,
separate evidence from status and remain operable without one company, model,
website, maintainer or paid account.

Scientific difficulty is not an excuse for an undefined process. Every live
station must state what is being tested, which inputs and rules are frozen,
what evidence is required, how a result fails, what tolerance applies and how
two other accountable humans can reproduce it without seeing the claimed
answer first.

## Worker bill of rights

Every human contributor has the right to:

1. enter through published evidence gates without a credential, affiliation,
   employment or payment requirement;
2. know a work order's scope, expected duration, compute or material budget,
   stop conditions, hazards, evidence boundary and credit class before work;
3. pause, decline or leave work without losing already earned attribution or
   being punished through rankings, access or scientific scoring;
4. keep the rights they lawfully hold and receive accurate, role-specific
   credit without assigning ownership to the Factory;
5. use a compatible agent or tool provider of their choice, including local
   tools, provided the declared station contract is satisfied;
6. receive an accessible route to instructions, evidence requirements,
   corrections, disputes and appeals;
7. challenge a technical or governance decision without retaliation; and
8. have personal data, identity evidence, hidden answers and confidential
   reports kept out of the public repository.

These rights do not create a right to bypass safety, licensing, identity,
independence or evidence gates. A failed gate rejects evidence, not a person's
worth or intelligence.

## Safe human–agent work

An agent is a tool operated under an accountable human's authority. Every
dispatched agent workload must have a bounded objective, allowed interfaces,
resource ceiling, termination condition and named human operator. Agents may
recommend continuation but may not silently expand their own authority,
spending, duration, data access or scientific standing.

The human operator may stop a run at any time. A stopped, blocked, no-gain or
unrunnable shift should be recorded honestly and can remain useful. The Factory
must not reward raw attempt volume, compute consumption or overnight endurance
as substitutes for evidence quality.

The universal dispatch-budget gate fails closed across time, compute, memory,
storage, process, spend, tool, filesystem, network and hazard controls. A runner
that cannot enforce every declared dimension receives a rejection, not partial
execution authority. The current built-in process runner remains rejected by
that universal gate; only a no-execution synthetic preflight is authorised.

## Scientific promises

The Factory promises that:

- hard gates fail closed and cannot be averaged away by a better headline
  metric;
- exact claims use exact checks, while statistical and physical tolerances are
  declared for the specific task;
- an author never validates their own claim;
- two reproductions mean two other accountable humans, not two accounts, two
  prompts or two agents owned by one person;
- validator evidence is committed before the hidden claim is revealed;
- disagreement opens diagnosis and appeal rather than promotion by majority
  vote;
- negative and disputed work remains searchable without exposing private
  evidence; and
- construction, code review, popularity, funding and commercial adoption do
  not create scientific standing.

## Rights, credit and economic neutrality

Contributors do not assign ownership to the Factory. Copyright, inventorship,
authorship, validation work, infrastructure work, external prizes and
commercial rights remain distinct records. The Factory may record declarations
and provenance but cannot decide legal ownership, patentability or freedom to
operate.

Scientific gates must not favour a contributor because they fund the Factory,
use a preferred provider, accept a commercial licence, waive a right or adopt
a patent pledge. Material sponsors, compute grants and conflicts of interest
must be disclosed where they could affect a workbench or decision.

The project's core contracts, verifier source, governance and construction
history must remain available under their declared open licences. Upstream
datasets and restricted benchmarks retain their own terms and are never made
open merely by being referenced here.

## Governance and appeal

Governance changes require a dedicated, reviewable change that names affected
invariants and migration consequences. No workbench contribution may weaken
global worker, rights or evidence protections as a side effect.

A person materially involved in a disputed contribution must disclose that
conflict and must not be the sole decision-maker on their own appeal. Sensitive
conduct, identity and security reports stay private; the public record should
state the applicable rule and outcome without unnecessarily exposing a
reporter.

The Factory is currently founder-maintained and has not completed independent
governance audit or multi-maintainer recovery. Those are published limitations,
not details to conceal behind a quality score.

## Measurement promise

The Factory will assess itself using
[`FACTORY_QUALITY_STANDARD.md`](FACTORY_QUALITY_STANDARD.md). Quality domains
are non-compensating gates: strength in one domain cannot erase failure in
another. Every assessment must identify the exact standard version, evidence
files, evidence hashes, unmet controls and certification boundary.

The Factory currently claims a construction foundation only. It claims no live
scientific certification, no independently audited quality profile and no
breakthrough produced by this Charter.

## Changing this Charter

Charter changes require a dedicated pull request, an explanation of whether any
right or scientific invariant is weakened, and an updated machine-readable
quality assessment. A change that weakens a protection must be stated plainly;
it must never be hidden as wording cleanup or station-specific maintenance.
