# 47 practical, benchmarkable research-factory workbenches

These are deliberately **problems with an external truth condition**, not open-ended “generate ideas” prompts. For every workbench, freeze the reference design, inputs, calibration, test conditions, scoring equation and cost year before a run; use public development cases plus hidden holdouts; require two independent human-owned reproductions; and promote only candidates that pass every hard safety/quality gate. A candidate can still be archived as a valuable typed negative result when it fails.

## Energy, power and thermal systems (12)

1. **Security-constrained electricity-grid dispatch.** **Pass gates:** On the ARPA-E cases, beat the frozen incumbent's production cost and runtime while satisfying AC power flow, equipment limits and every specified contingency; zero involuntary load shedding unless the case is itself infeasible. **Economic guardrail:** Report annual operating savings net of compute, communications and integration; a cheaper schedule that weakens reliability is an automatic fail. **Primary benchmark:** [ARPA-E Grid Optimization Competition](https://arpa-e.energy.gov/programs-and-initiatives/view-all-programs/go-competition).

2. **Higher-efficiency photovoltaic inverter.** **Pass gates:** Independent-lab DC-to-AC tests at the CEC protocol's 18 voltage/load points; improve weighted efficiency while staying inside harmonic distortion, power-factor, thermal, overload and protection limits. **Economic guardrail:** Lower lifetime $/delivered AC kWh after inverter cost, standby loss and replacement risk—not merely higher peak efficiency. **Primary protocol:** [Sandia/CEC inverter test protocol](https://pvpmc.sandia.gov/modeling-guide/dc-to-ac-conversion/cec-inverter-test-protocol/).

3. **Grid-supporting DER inverter controls.** **Pass gates:** On hardware-in-the-loop and then a bounded microgrid, improve voltage/frequency regulation and transient recovery; pass ride-through, anti-islanding, interoperability, power-quality and abnormal-grid tests. **Economic guardrail:** Value of avoided curtailment/outages must exceed added sensing, certification and control hardware cost per kW. **Primary test basis:** [Sandia Distributed Energy Technologies Laboratory](https://www.sandia.gov/working-with-sandia/technology-deployment-centers/distributed-energy-technology-laboratory-detl/).

4. **Longer-lived emerging photovoltaic module.** **Pass gates:** Use identical aperture, mounting, preconditioning and outdoor/stress protocols; improve lifetime energy yield and degradation rate with no safety, encapsulation or repeatability failure across replicate modules. **Economic guardrail:** Reduce levelized $/kWh after module, balance-of-system, yield loss and replacement costs. **Primary protocol/data:** [Sandia PACT protocols](https://pvpact.sandia.gov/publications-and-protocols/).

5. **Wind-turbine controller that raises useful energy without consuming fatigue life.** **Pass gates:** Across frozen OpenFAST wind/wave/turbulence cases, increase annual energy capture while not worsening ultimate loads, damage-equivalent fatigue loads, overspeed, tower/blade clearance or actuator duty; then controller-in-the-loop confirmation. **Economic guardrail:** LCOE saving after control hardware, maintenance and any component-life penalty. **Primary benchmark platform:** [NREL OpenFAST](https://www.nrel.gov/wind/nwtc/openfast).

6. **Faster EV-battery charging with preserved life.** **Pass gates:** Under the current USABC procedure, reduce charge time while meeting energy, power, resistance, temperature-uniformity, abuse and end-of-life requirements over blinded replicate cells/modules; record all rests and thermal conditions. **Economic guardrail:** Minimise total $/vehicle-km or $/delivered lifetime kWh including charger power, cooling and accelerated pack replacement. **Primary protocol:** [USABC EV Battery Test Manual](https://uscar.org/download/247/usabc-manuals/16391/electric-vehicle-test-manual.pdf).

7. **Lower-cost long-duration electricity storage.** **Pass gates:** At a declared duration and duty cycle, improve round-trip efficiency and capacity retention while passing response-time, self-discharge, cycle/calendar-life and safety tests on the same device. **Economic guardrail:** Lower levelized cost of storage under frozen financing, utilisation, augmentation and disposal assumptions; no cost claim based on excluding balance-of-plant. **Primary baseline:** [DOE Energy Storage Cost and Performance Assessment](https://www.energy.gov/sites/default/files/2022-09/2022%20Grid%20Energy%20Storage%20Technology%20Cost%20and%20Performance%20Assessment.pdf).

8. **Cold-climate heat pump.** **Pass gates:** Meet or beat rated capacity, COP, turndown and seasonal efficiency at the DOE challenge temperatures, with acceptable defrost/cycling behaviour, comfort, noise, refrigerant leakage and low-GWP refrigerant. **Economic guardrail:** Lower levelized cost of delivered heat than the frozen incumbent after installed cost, backup heat, electricity tariffs and maintenance. **Primary specification:** [DOE Cold Climate Heat Pump Challenge](https://www.energy.gov/cmei/buildings/cchp-technology-challenge-specifications).

9. **Building HVAC supervisory controller.** **Pass gates:** Beat the embedded controller across hidden BOPTEST buildings, weather, occupancy, tariff and forecast-error cases on energy, peak demand and cost while respecting comfort/IAQ bounds and actuator cycling; field pilot only after simulation. **Economic guardrail:** Verified bill/maintenance savings must repay sensors, commissioning, licence and integration within a declared maximum payback. **Primary benchmark:** [DOE BOPTEST](https://www.energy.gov/cmei/buildings/boptest-building-operations-testing-framework).

10. **Compact, low-pressure-drop heat exchanger.** **Pass gates:** At matched fluids, inlet states and duties, improve heat-transfer effectiveness per volume/mass while not increasing pressure drop, leakage, fouling, corrosion or thermal-cycle failure; independently calorimeter-tested. **Economic guardrail:** Lower $/kW recovered and lifecycle pumping/fan cost, not heat transfer alone. **Primary target example:** [DOE advanced heat-exchanger performance targets](https://www.energy.gov/cmei/buildings/articles/advanced-serpentine-heat-exchangers-minimize-number-joints-and-leakage).

11. **High-temperature thermal-energy store.** **Pass gates:** Improve usable energy density and demonstrate charge/discharge power, heat loss, round-trip/exergetic efficiency, cycling, containment and corrosion under a frozen temperature profile. **Economic guardrail:** Target <$15/kWh-thermal and include insulation, vessels, heat exchangers, replacement media and parasitic loads. **Primary targets:** [DOE CSP thermal-storage R&D](https://www.energy.gov/cmei/systems/thermal-storage-rd-csp-systems).

12. **More efficient, durable PEM electrolyser.** **Pass gates:** On the same stack/system meet current-density/voltage, kWh/kg-H2, purity/crossover, dynamic duty-cycle, degradation and safety gates simultaneously. **Economic guardrail:** Lower levelized $/kg-H2 including stack replacement, water treatment, compression and realistic electricity utilisation; do not trade scarce PGM loading for hidden cost. **Primary targets:** [DOE PEM electrolysis targets](https://stage.energy.gov/cmei/fuels/technical-targets-proton-exchange-membrane-electrolysis).

## Manufacturing, materials and recycling (12)

13. **Metal-AM residual-stress and distortion control.** **Pass gates:** Blindly predict and then reduce NIST bridge-part residual strains and post-cut deflections, with unchanged alloy/feedstock and scan envelope; final coupons must also meet geometry, porosity and tensile requirements. **Economic guardrail:** Lower $/qualified part after build time, scrap, support removal, heat treatment and metrology. **Primary benchmark:** [NIST AM-Bench 2022-01](https://www.nist.gov/ambench/amb2022-01-benchmark-measurements-and-challenge-problems).

14. **Metal-AM fatigue-life improvement.** **Pass gates:** On held-out AM-Bench specimens, improve the S-N curve/lower confidence bound without failing density, geometry, residual stress, tensile or repeat-build variability gates; enough specimens for a predeclared statistical comparison. **Economic guardrail:** Lower $/fatigue-qualified component including HIP/heat treatment, inspection, energy and yield loss. **Primary benchmark:** [NIST AM-Bench 2025 challenge descriptions](https://www.nist.gov/ambench/short-descriptions-2025-benchmarks).

15. **Five-axis CNC accuracy/productivity optimisation.** **Pass gates:** On fixed test artefacts and materials, reduce volumetric/contouring error and surface roughness while not increasing cycle time, energy, tool wear, chatter or nonconforming parts; repeat after thermal soak and on a second machine. **Economic guardrail:** Lower $/conforming part including tool, machine-hour, energy, inspection and scrap. **Primary test basis:** [NIST standardized five-axis performance tests](https://www.nist.gov/publications/virtual-machine-tool-evaluation-standardized-5-axis-performance-tests).

16. **Sheet-metal springback compensation.** **Pass gates:** On hidden alloys/thicknesses and strain paths, reduce final geometry error under the ASTM/NIST cup-test framework while meeting thinning, wrinkling, crack and residual-stress limits. **Economic guardrail:** Demonstrably reduce die rework/try-outs and scrap enough to cover added characterization and computation. **Primary protocol:** [NIST springback programme / ASTM E2462 basis](https://www.nist.gov/programs-projects/springback).

17. **Robust weld schedule with less distortion and rework.** **Pass gates:** Freeze alloy, joint and load case; qualify procedure and operator/automation, then pass visual/NDE, tensile/bend, metallography, defect, residual-distortion and fatigue gates across independent coupon batches. **Economic guardrail:** Lower $/accepted joint after consumables, shielding, energy, cycle time, NDE, repair and fatigue-life cost. **Primary requirements:** [NASA-STD-5006 welding standard](https://standards.nasa.gov/standard/NASA/NASA-STD-5006).

18. **Lower-energy injection-moulding cycle.** **Pass gates:** For a frozen mould and resin/reference blend, cut kWh and cycle time per part while holding mass, critical dimensions/warpage, surface, strength and defect rate inside limits across machine and lot changes. **Economic guardrail:** Lower $/good part after cooling, clamping, maintenance and scrap; no gain from simply under-packing the part. **Primary measurement basis:** [NIST injection-moulding energy profiling](https://tsapps.nist.gov/publication/get_pdf.cfm?pub_id=905587).

19. **Low-carbon concrete that retains service performance.** **Pass gates:** Beat baseline kgCO2e/m3 while meeting declared slump/air, early and 28/56-day strength, shrinkage, permeability/chloride, freeze-thaw and durability gates over multiple material lots. **Economic guardrail:** Equal or lower lifecycle $ per structural service-year, including cure time, labour, section size and maintenance—not cement cost alone. **Primary programme:** [NIST green-concrete measurement science](https://www.nist.gov/programs-projects/measurement-science-assure-performance-green-concretes-project).

20. **Longer-life corrosion protection for structural steel.** **Pass gates:** Against A36/A588 controls under the same cyclic salt/humidity exposure, reduce mass/thickness loss while passing adhesion, impact, slip/creep, UV/weathering and repairability gates. **Economic guardrail:** Lower discounted $/protected m2-year including surface preparation, application shutdowns and recoating. **Primary protocol example:** [FHWA cyclic corrosion testing](https://www.fhwa.dot.gov/publications/research/infrastructure/bridge/11062/005.cfm).

21. **Direct recycling of lithium-ion cathode material.** **Pass gates:** From blinded mixed-age feed, report mass balance; recover cathode at required composition/purity and demonstrate capacity, rate, impedance and cycle retention versus virgin control, with safe effluent/residue accounting. **Economic guardrail:** EverBatt net $/kg cathode and energy/GHG must beat the frozen hydro/pyro/virgin alternatives at realistic throughput. **Primary models/programme:** [DOE ReCell Center](https://recellcenter.org/) and [Argonne EverBatt](https://everbatt.amd.anl.gov/).

22. **Higher-purity plastic sorting.** **Pass gates:** On hidden, weighed post-consumer mixtures spanning resin, colour, additives, labels, dirt and fluorescence, improve both purity and recovery at production throughput; publish the complete reject/residue mass balance. **Economic guardrail:** Higher net value/ton after equipment, labour, energy, false rejects and disposal—not classification accuracy alone. **Primary measurement programme:** [NIST plastic composition and sorting](https://www.nist.gov/circular-economy/improving-composition-analysis-and-sorting-plastics).

23. **Stronger products from mixed recycled polyolefins.** **Pass gates:** Across blinded HDPE/LDPE/PP ratios and contaminant lots, meet melt-flow/processability plus tensile, impact, creep, ageing and repeat-recycle gates versus the incumbent compatibilisation process. **Economic guardrail:** Lower $/functional-property unit than virgin or down-cycled material after additives and processing energy. **Primary basis:** [NIST polymers/plastics circularity research](https://www.nist.gov/circular-economy/research-areas/polymers-plastics).

24. **Rare-earth magnet recovery and remanufacture.** **Pass gates:** From a specified mixture of end-of-life products, close the mass balance and improve magnet/rare-earth recovery, purity, throughput and remanufactured magnetic performance while passing hazardous-waste and worker-safety gates. **Economic guardrail:** Net $/kg magnet-equivalent below virgin supply after collection, disassembly, separation, reagent and waste costs. **Primary programme:** [DOE/ORNL rare-earth magnet recovery process](https://www.energy.gov/technologycommercialization/articles/ornl-licenses-rare-earth-magnet-recycling-process-momentum).

## Water, agriculture, environment and civil infrastructure (11)

25. **Lower-energy reverse-osmosis desalination train.** **Pass gates:** At frozen feed salinity/temperature and product-quality targets, reduce specific energy while maintaining recovery, flux, salt/contaminant rejection, fouling/cleaning frequency, membrane life and brine accounting; pilot follows WaterTAP blind cases. **Economic guardrail:** Lower levelized $/m3 including intake, pretreatment, energy recovery, replacements, post-treatment and brine disposal. **Primary benchmark:** [DOE-funded WaterTAP seawater-RO flowsheet](https://watertap.readthedocs.io/en/stable/technical_reference/flowsheets/seawater_RO_desalination.html).

26. **Cheaper PFAS removal from drinking water.** **Pass gates:** Using EPA analytical methods and blinded natural-water matrices, keep all specified PFAS below the predeclared limit through breakthrough; record bed volumes, co-contaminants, waste/regeneration fate and measurement uncertainty. **Economic guardrail:** Lower lifecycle $/m3 in the matching EPA WBS model, including residual handling and media replacement. **Primary methods/cost basis:** [EPA PFAS treatment and cost document](https://www.epa.gov/system/files/documents/2024-04/2024-pfas-tech-cost_final-508.pdf).

27. **Wastewater nitrogen/phosphorus recovery.** **Pass gates:** On standard BSM2/influent profiles and then duplicate pilots, meet effluent COD/N/P limits while increasing saleable N/P recovery, energy neutrality and process stability; close C/N/P and solids mass balances. **Economic guardrail:** Lower net $/m3 after capex/O&M, aeration, chemicals, sludge and realistic recovered-product revenue. **Primary benchmark family:** [WaterTAP flowsheets, including Benchmark Simulation Model No. 2](https://watertap.readthedocs.io/en/1.5.0/technical_reference/flowsheets/).

28. **Water-network leak localisation and pressure control.** **Pass gates:** On hidden EPANET networks with seeded leaks/sensor faults, improve leak-location precision/recall, detection delay and water lost while maintaining minimum pressure, fire flow and water age/quality; confirm on a bounded physical network. **Economic guardrail:** Annual value of saved water, pumping and damage minus sensors/telemetry/repairs must be positive. **Primary platform:** [EPA EPANET](https://www.epa.gov/water-research/epanet).

29. **Stormwater and combined-sewer overflow control.** **Pass gates:** Across frozen observed and hidden design storms in SWMM, reduce flooding, overflow volume/frequency and pollutant load without shifting flooding downstream; test actuator failure and forecast error. **Economic guardrail:** Lower annualised $ per m3 overflow avoided and expected damage, including land, maintenance and control hardware. **Primary platform:** [EPA SWMM](https://www.epa.gov/water-research/storm-water-management-model-swmm).

30. **Precision irrigation scheduling.** **Pass gates:** In randomized replicated plots, reduce applied water and drainage/runoff while preserving marketable yield/quality and staying within crop water-stress and soil-salinity bounds across at least two seasons/sites. **Economic guardrail:** Improve farm gross margin/ha after water, pumping, sensors, communications and labour. **Primary method evidence:** [USDA-ARS water-stress irrigation scheduling](https://www.ars.usda.gov/research/publications/publication/?seqNo115=338063).

31. **Field-specific nitrogen-rate and timing optimisation.** **Pass gates:** Under replicated strips and blinded seasons, preserve yield/quality while reducing surplus N, post-harvest nitrate, leaching/runoff and N2O; comply with the local 4R/NRCS nutrient plan. **Economic guardrail:** Improve profit/ha after fertiliser, sensing and application while reporting $/kg N loss avoided. **Primary standard:** [USDA-NRCS Nutrient Management 590](https://www.nrcs.usda.gov/resources/guides-and-instructions/nutrient-management-ac-590-conservation-practice-standard).

32. **Remote methane leak detection and quantification.** **Pass gates:** In blind controlled releases spanning flow, wind, terrain, backgrounds and interferents, improve probability of detection, localization error and quantified-flow bias/uncertainty; then independent field verification. **Economic guardrail:** Recovered-gas and avoided-emission value must exceed sensor, survey and false-dispatch cost per site-year. **Primary practice:** [NIST common practices for quantifying methane plumes](https://www.nist.gov/publications/common-practices-quantifying-methane-emissions-plumes-detected-remote-sensing).

33. **Reliable low-cost PM2.5/ozone air sensor.** **Pass gates:** At least three units complete EPA base and enhanced tests against FRM/FEM references across sites, humidity, temperature and concentration; beat targets for precision, bias, R2, RMSE, drift and data completeness. **Economic guardrail:** Lower $/valid measurement-site-year including calibration, communications, maintenance and replacement. **Primary protocol:** [EPA air-sensor performance targets and tests](https://www.epa.gov/air-sensor-toolbox/air-sensor-performance-targets-and-testing-protocols).

34. **Longer-lasting, lower-carbon high-RAP asphalt.** **Pass gates:** Increase reclaimed asphalt content while passing balanced-mix rutting, fatigue/thermal cracking, moisture and volumetric criteria after ageing; validate with a test strip and later field/LTPP follow-up. **Economic guardrail:** Lower lifecycle $/lane-km and kgCO2e/lane-km including rejuvenator, plant energy, maintenance and service life. **Primary method:** [FHWA Balanced Mix Design](https://www.fhwa.dot.gov/pavement/asphalt/amdbmd/).

35. **Bridge structural-health monitoring with fewer false alarms.** **Pass gates:** On seeded/blinded damage and environmental/load changes, improve probability of detection, localisation and severity error with predeclared false-alarm/missed-damage bounds; sensor calibration, uptime and uncertainty are hard gates. **Economic guardrail:** Expected inspection/downtime/failure-risk saving must exceed installed and lifecycle sensor/data cost. **Primary test/field basis:** [FHWA structural-health monitoring practice](https://www.fhwa.dot.gov/publications/research/infrastructure/structures/bridge/09040/002.cfm).

## Robotics, control and transport (12)

36. **Agile robotic kitting and assembly.** **Pass gates:** Beat the frozen ARIAC competitor across hidden orders and repeated trials on correct kits/assemblies, completion time and recovery from faulty/dropped parts, sensor blackout and robot malfunction; zero collision/illegal-action penalties. **Economic guardrail:** Lower $/correct order after robot, sensors, changeover, supervision and downtime. **Primary benchmark:** [NIST ARIAC](https://pages.nist.gov/ARIAC_docs/en/2024.1.0/index.html).

37. **More capable, repeatable robotic grasping.** **Pass gates:** On hidden objects/materials/poses, improve successful grasp-and-place rate, cycle time, strength and in-hand pose error while limiting damage, drops and peak forces; use independent ground-truth metrology and repeated trials. **Economic guardrail:** Lower $/successful safe pick after gripper, sensing, compute, maintenance and product damage. **Primary test methods:** [NIST grasping performance metrics](https://www.nist.gov/el/intelligent-systems-division-73500/robotic-grasping-and-manipulation-assembly/grasping).

38. **Accurate low-cost in-situ industrial-robot calibration.** **Pass gates:** Across hidden workspace poses, payloads and temperatures, reduce absolute position/orientation error and uncertainty versus baseline calibration, retain repeatability, and reproduce against an independent laser tracker. **Economic guardrail:** Value of fewer fixtures/rework and faster commissioning must exceed calibration hardware and downtime. **Primary measurement basis:** [NIST in-situ robot-accuracy method](https://www.nist.gov/publications/efficiently-improving-and-quantifying-robot-accuracy-situ).

39. **Autonomous mobile-robot navigation and docking.** **Pass gates:** Under ASTM/NIST defined-area and docking tests, improve path deviation, localization, completion time and docking error across narrow aisles, ramps, load shifts, dynamic obstacles and forced delocalisation; no contact or safety-zone breach. **Economic guardrail:** Lower $/successful pallet/task-km after fleet size, charging, mapping, supervision and collision/downtime risk. **Primary test programme:** [NIST mobile-robot test methods](https://www.nist.gov/programs-projects/mobility-performance-robotic-systems).

40. **Predictive maintenance with useful warning, not alarm spam.** **Pass gates:** On NASA/NIST hidden run-to-failure trajectories, improve remaining-useful-life error and calibrated uncertainty while meeting minimum warning time, false-alarm and missed-failure limits; prospective shadow-mode plant test required before acting. **Economic guardrail:** Expected avoided downtime/failure minus false maintenance, sensors and integration must beat scheduled/reactive maintenance. **Primary benchmark:** [NASA Prognostics Data Repository](https://www.nasa.gov/intelligent-systems-division/discovery-and-systems-health/pcoe/pcoe-data-set-repository/).

41. **Automatic emergency braking that avoids crashes without false stops.** **Pass gates:** Under the applicable NHTSA vehicle/pedestrian scenarios and hidden variations in speed, overlap, lighting/weather and target type, avoid contact or meet residual-speed limits; hard caps on false activation, jerk, stopping stability and diagnostic failure. **Economic guardrail:** Safety benefit/expected crash-cost reduction must exceed sensors, actuators, validation and false-event costs. **Primary procedures:** [NHTSA NCAP crash-avoidance test procedures](https://www.nhtsa.gov/ratings/resources-related-nhtsas-new-car-assessment-program).

42. **Robust lane centering/lane-departure prevention.** **Pass gates:** Across frozen and hidden road curvature, markings, speed, lighting, weather and sensor-degradation cases, reduce lateral/path error and departures while meeting driver-override, oscillation, minimum-risk and false-intervention limits; track then supervised-road validation. **Economic guardrail:** Collision-risk benefit must exceed hardware, calibration and maintenance without requiring unaffordable map/sensor infrastructure. **Primary test framework:** [NHTSA automated-driving reports and testable scenarios](https://www.nhtsa.gov/automated-vehicles-safety/published-reports-and-documents).

43. **Adaptive traffic-signal control.** **Pass gates:** On held-out weeks/incidents and then a controlled corridor, reduce person-delay, queue spillback and stops while not worsening pedestrian wait, transit reliability, emergency response or crash-surrogate measures; compare with the current timing plan. **Economic guardrail:** Annual travel-time/fuel/emission benefits minus controllers, detection, communications and retiming/maintenance must be positive. **Primary measures:** [FHWA Automated Traffic Signal Performance Measures](https://highways.dot.gov/media/89151).

44. **Energy- and emissions-aware delivery routing.** **Pass gates:** For identical jobs, time windows, capacity and service constraints, reduce measured or validated route energy/CO2e versus current dispatch while preserving on-time completion and driver rules; hidden demand/traffic days prevent benchmark overfit. **Economic guardrail:** Lower all-in $/delivered stop including fuel/energy, wages, vehicle wear, lateness and compute. **Primary physical model:** [EPA MOVES5](https://www.epa.gov/moves/latest-version-motor-vehicle-emission-simulator-moves) (second-by-second route inputs are supported).

45. **Safe, fuel-saving cooperative vehicle platooning.** **Pass gates:** On closed track across gaps, grades, cut-ins, braking, packet loss and mixed loading, reduce fuel/energy while meeting string stability, stopping-distance, lateral control, fallback and communications-loss limits. **Economic guardrail:** Fleet fuel saving minus V2X/control hardware, driver/operations constraints, maintenance and added risk must be positive. **Primary dataset:** [USDOT CACC platooning test data](https://data.transportation.gov/Automobiles/Test-Data-of-Proof-of-Concept-Vehicle-Platooning-B/wpek-zziu).

46. **Vehicle powertrain and energy-management controller.** **Pass gates:** Across standard and hidden real-world duty cycles, reduce fuel/electricity use while meeting acceleration, gradeability, range, battery SOC/temperature/life, emissions and drivability constraints; dynamometer confirmation after FASTSim. **Economic guardrail:** Lower total cost of ownership per km after component sizing, battery life and maintenance, not just cycle efficiency. **Primary benchmark/model:** [NREL FASTSim](https://www.nrel.gov/transportation/fastsim).

47. **Precision robotic pesticide sprayer.** **Pass gates:** In replicated orchard/vineyard rows with hidden canopy/pest distributions, increase target deposition/coverage and reduce off-target drift and chemical use while maintaining pest control, crop injury, operator/bystander safety and navigation limits. **Economic guardrail:** Improve grower margin/ha after robot, chemical, labour, charging, maintenance and downtime; no credit for chemical reduction that lowers efficacy. **Primary field-test basis:** [USDA-ARS unmanned-sprayer assessment](https://www.ars.usda.gov/research/publications/publication/?seqNo115=416190).
