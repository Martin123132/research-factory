# Major breakthrough protocol

This protocol applies to extraordinary claims such as a Clay Mathematics
Institute problem, a foundational theorem, or a practical result with unusually
large scientific or commercial consequences. It adds scrutiny; it does not
replace the station's objective gates.

## 1. Freeze the claim

Create an immutable package containing the exact claim, definitions,
assumptions, code or proof, dependencies, environment, artifact hashes and
timestamp. Later corrections become new versions rather than silent edits.

If the package may contain a patentable invention, stop before repository
upload and follow [PATENTS_AND_PUBLIC_DISCLOSURE.md](PATENTS_AND_PUBLIC_DISCLOSURE.md).
The current Factory does not accept confidential breakthrough packages.

## 2. Pass the ordinary workbench

The candidate must pass every hard gate under the locked contract. Exact claims
use exact or formal checks; statistical evidence cannot be converted into a
proof claim. A large score cannot override an invalid artifact or missing gate.

## 3. Audit provenance and prior work

Record every material source, predecessor, negative result and dependency.
Search for earlier publication and independent discovery. Create the role ledger
required by [CREDIT_AUTHORSHIP_AND_PRIZES.md](CREDIT_AUTHORSHIP_AND_PRIZES.md).

## 4. Run blind independent reproduction

Two different humans, neither the author nor each other, independently run the
locked package and commit their results before reveal. A split verdict opens
diagnosis and conflict-free human review; it cannot promote by majority vote.

For a proof, reproduction includes line-by-line mathematical scrutiny and any
required formal verification, not merely rerunning a notebook that prints the
claimed conclusion.

## 5. Run separate rights and safety gates

Rights, publication safety, research ethics, biosecurity, cybersecurity and
physical safety are reviewed independently of scientific correctness. A valid
result may be held from distribution for a specific safety or rights reason,
but the reason must not be disguised as a failed measurement.

## 6. External scrutiny

Release a citable package and invite qualified hostile review. Where a journal,
institute, regulator or prize body has its own process, comply with that process;
Factory promotion does not substitute for it.

## 7. Attribute and announce cautiously

Publish the evidence state, unresolved objections, role ledger and rights
declarations. Use `verified by the Factory protocol` only for the exact frozen
package and contract version. Do not announce a prize winner, patent owner or
sole inventor unless the competent external process has established it.

## Emergency correction

Any material flaw opens a linked dispute immediately. Promotion can be
suspended while the immutable prior record remains visible. Corrections,
retractions and restored status are separately timestamped events.
