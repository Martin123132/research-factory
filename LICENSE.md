# Research Factory licensing

This is a multi-licensed repository. There is deliberately no blanket licence
over every present and future research artifact.

The authoritative file-by-file map is [`REUSE.toml`](REUSE.toml). The unmodified
standard licence texts are stored in [`LICENSES/`](LICENSES/):

- Factory software, schemas and automation: Apache License 2.0;
- authored documentation and descriptive station material: Creative Commons
  Attribution 4.0 International;
- Factory-created factual records and synthetic public data: CC0 1.0; and
- the bundled Geist font subsets: SIL Open Font License 1.1.

Copyright remains with each contributor or upstream rightsholder. These
licences grant reuse permissions; they do not assign ownership to the Research
Factory or its maintainers.

Candidate research artifacts do not inherit a repository-wide licence. They
must use the separate handling rules in
[`candidate_artifacts/README.md`](candidate_artifacts/README.md). An artifact
without an explicit accepted licence is metadata-only and is not copied into
the repository.

Names and logos are not licensed as trade marks. Third-party components retain
their original terms; see [`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).
